Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-11

This policy describes when and how you can cancel your HoshimiLive subscription and request a refund. Payments are processed by our merchant-of-record partners (Paddle and Lemon Squeezy), so refunds are issued back to your original payment method through them.

Cancellation — anytime

You can cancel your Starlight Monthly or Celestial Annual subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep full access until then, and you will not be billed again.

To cancel:

  • Use the "Manage subscription" link in any payment confirmation email from Paddle or Lemon Squeezy, or
  • Email contact@hoshimilive.com from the address you used to subscribe.

14-day refund guarantee (new subscribers)

If you subscribe to a paid plan for the first time and are not satisfied, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase. This applies to:

  • Starlight Monthly — full refund of your first month's charge
  • Celestial Annual — full refund of your first annual charge

To request a refund, email contact@hoshimilive.com with the subject line "Refund request" and include the email address used at checkout plus your Paddle/Lemon Squeezy order ID. Refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days back to your original payment method.

Annual plan — prorated refunds

If you cancel a Celestial Annual subscription after the 14-day window, you may request a prorated refund for unused full months remaining in your subscription, minus a $10 processing fee. For example, if you cancel 4 months into a 12-month subscription, you may receive a refund for 8 unused months × (annual price ÷ 12) − $10.

Monthly subscriptions are not eligible for prorated refunds after the 14-day window — you simply retain access until the end of the current billing month.

Not refundable

  • One-time in-service purchases (e.g., special seasonal reading packs) after they have been consumed.
  • Charges older than 180 days.
  • Accounts terminated for violation of our Terms of Service (for example, fraud, chargeback abuse, or automated scraping).

Chargebacks

If you have a billing issue, please contact us first at contact@hoshimilive.com — we resolve nearly all disputes within one business day. Filing a chargeback without contacting us may result in your account being suspended.
